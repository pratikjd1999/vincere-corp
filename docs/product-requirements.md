# Product Requirements: Vincere Corp Website

## Product

Vincere Corp Private Limited is an IT company based in Pune, Maharashtra. The website presents its capability to undertake government tenders and private technology/software projects.

## Goal

Create an immersive, futuristic, responsive public website that explains the company, its service capabilities, sectors served, and compliance credentials, and gives prospective clients a clear way to make contact.

## Brand message

- Primary themes: **Modern digital solutions** and **Build the future digitally**.
- Use a text wordmark, `VINCERE CORP`, until an approved logo is supplied.

## Users

- Government officials and procurement stakeholders assessing a technology partner.
- Businesses seeking a website, application, or other software-development partner.

## Version 1 scope

- Public informational website only; no login, signup, dashboard, CMS, or backend.
- Pages: Home, About / Who We Are, Services, Industries / Development Areas, Certifications & Compliance, Contact Us.
- Light and dark modes.
- Responsive experience for phones, laptops, and larger screens.
- Contact information may show Pune, Maharashtra. Public email address and phone number are pending; the contact form is deferred until a backend/email service is approved.
- Certificate categories may be displayed, but certificate numbers, documents, dates, and other private details must remain non-public until explicitly approved.

## Service areas

- Websites
- Web applications
- Mobile applications
- Government tender solutions
- UI/UX design
- Cloud solutions
- AI automation
- Cybersecurity
- Maintenance and support

## Initial industries

- Government & Public Sector
- Small and Medium Businesses
- Startups
- Education
- Healthcare

## Non-functional requirements

- Mobile-first, responsive layout.
- Use intentional 3D scenes, scroll-led storytelling, smooth page/section transitions, and refined micro-interactions to create a premium futuristic experience.
- Motion must be purposeful, not decorative: it must not block reading, navigation, or calls to action.
- All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Provide an equally complete non-animated experience.
- Treat 3D as progressive enhancement: offer a performant static or simplified fallback on constrained devices, unsupported browsers, and slow connections.
- WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility target.
- Search-engine friendly semantic content and metadata.
- Fast, polished experience with an aspirational Lighthouse target of 95+ for performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO on representative public pages.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; animation must support content rather than obstruct it.

## Explicitly deferred

- Theme customisation through uploaded images/colour extraction.
- Backend, database, form delivery, authentication, payment, tender portal, and CMS.
- Publishing private certificate or incorporation information.
