# Design Direction

## Intent

Create an immersive, futuristic technology-company experience: confident, clear, and credible for both public-sector and private-sector buyers. A prominent **Check what we can do** call to action introduces a 3D, scroll-led capability experience. The reference study suggests an editorial, full-width presentation with structured service storytelling, strong typography, intentional imagery, and decisive calls to action. It is inspiration only; do not copy its design, assets, or wording.

## Foundation

- Support light and dark modes, with the selected preference persisted locally.
- Start with a restrained neutral palette plus one accent colour, to be selected after brand discovery.
- Use generous whitespace, clear content hierarchy, spatial depth, smooth transitions, and subtle-to-confident motion.
- Use real/approved imagery only. Until imagery is supplied, use a deliberate abstract visual system rather than misleading stock depictions of projects or clients.
- Use consistent cards, buttons, section labels, navigation, and footer across all pages.

## Responsive rules

- Design from 320px upward.
- Navigation becomes an accessible mobile menu on small screens.
- No horizontal scrolling or hover-only functionality.
- Touch targets must be at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- On reduced-motion or low-capability devices, present static spatial compositions in place of continuous 3D and scroll animations.

## Compliance display

Reserve four or five intentionally neutral compliance-card positions to support page layout. They must use labels such as `Credential details available on request` and must not imply a certification, approval, or registration that has not been confirmed. Do not show scans, registration numbers, QR codes, dates, seals, or legal claims without approval.
